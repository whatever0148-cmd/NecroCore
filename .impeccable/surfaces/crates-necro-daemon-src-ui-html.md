## Scope and mode
NecroCore Observatory (local daemon UI and the hosted GitHub Pages copy). Mode: Operate. The visitor is a COBOL maintainer, and for Kiroween a judge with a few minutes. Job: see what is rotten in an old program, have Kiro fix it, and see the proof.

## Direction contract
THESIS: A working analysis tool set in the light of a 1978 bank machine room at midnight. It refuses the category default, a cold grey dark dashboard with one neon accent and card grids.
OWN-WORLD: Warm machine-room black ground (brown-black, never cold grey), amber phosphor for live text, focus and the proof readout, rust for structure (rails, rules, selected states), oxide red only for a live finding, bone paper for printed reports, green phosphor reserved for the PhantomArcade cabinet. Type: VT323 for display and readouts, IBM Plex Mono for code and numbers, a plain system sans for prose and controls. Controls stay standard web controls.
STORY: The visitor picks a 1978-1985 money program, sees its rot on its own lines, watches Kiro's recorded fix plan, and reads 10,000 of 10,000 inputs matching. They believe a change to old money code can be trusted when it is proven.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Top bar with the NecroCore wordmark and three tabs. Left two thirds: the program listing on a warm amber-lit screen with the haunted lines visible without scrolling. Right third: the counts (Ghost, Vampire, Zombie with their technical terms) and the next action. The proof readout sits under the counts.
FORM: Operate shell with one signature move. Signature interaction: haunted lines in the listing. A Ghost line dims and flickers, a Zombie loop's lines keep a slow scroll shimmer, a Vampire line swells; clicking any of them jumps the graph and the finding. Motion grammar: phosphor fade-in on load, exponential ease-out, all off under prefers-reduced-motion. Direction pinned by the user after research, which beats the roll. Roll recorded: seed:a61c443d121f0d30. Challenger verdicts, all declined on audience identification and product clarity: warm consumer app surface (kept its discipline: the solid amber fill appears only where an action can be taken, the primary button, and the proof readout is the only other amber field), vertical video feed, zoo guide map, botanical folio, star atlas (kept: finding severity reads from size and weight before colour), data-sublime field.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
